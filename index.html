One link in the group chat. The game fills itself.

Share the link to WhatsApp. People tap I’m in and take a space; when it’s full they join the waiting list, and move up if someone drops out.

  • Everyone who says yes joins your regulars for next week
  • Split the pitch fee by however many play
  • See who’s paid, and what each person owes over the season
THE BITS ORGANISERS ASK FOR

Less chasing, more playing

Fair teams, chosen for youDayli learns from past scores and splits players into two even sides. Or mix them up at random.
Who owes whatAn “Owed to you” list across every game, with a one-tap nudge and the dates it’s from.
Waiting list that runs itselfFull up? People queue. Someone drops out, the next person is in and told straight away.
Reminders and nudgesDayli reminds people who haven’t answered and who haven’t paid, so you don’t have to.
